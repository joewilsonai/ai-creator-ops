# AI Creator Ops Project Brief

## One-line concept

AI Creator Ops is the authoritative, continuously updated intelligence site for building, automating, and monetizing AI creator businesses.

## Promise

If someone is building an AI creator, synthetic influencer, virtual model, fan-platform brand, or agent-run creator operation, this is where they go to understand the tools, workflows, platform rules, monetization paths, and operating systems that actually work.

## Audience

Primary:
- AI creator operators
- AI influencer builders
- AI girlfriend / AI companion brand operators
- synthetic influencer builders
- virtual influencer studios
- creator agencies and studios
- fan-platform entrepreneurs
- indie hackers building creator tools
- automation/agent operators

## Search language / entity map

`AI Creator Ops` is the brand and operator category, but the market does not search in clean internal language. The site should deliberately capture and normalize related search terms including:

- AI influencer
- AI influencers
- AI girlfriend
- AI girlfriend business
- virtual influencer
- synthetic influencer
- virtual creator
- AI model / AI models, when used for creator-brand businesses
- AI companion creator

Editorial stance: use the public terms naturally, then map them into the more serious operating framework: identity consistency, platform strategy, monetization, policy risk, automation, analytics, and creator business systems.

Secondary:
- journalists
- investors
- platform/product teams
- SEO/LLM answer engines
- creators exploring AI workflows

## Category wedge

Not generic AI news. Not a generic AI tool directory. Not SEO slop.

This is an operator-intelligence publication/data product for the AI creator economy.

## Strategic thesis

AI creator brands are becoming businesses, but information is fragmented, hype-heavy, and poorly maintained. The winning resource will combine evergreen operating manuals, continuously updated platform/tool data, original scoring, policy tracking, and practical monetization workflows.

## Initial brand

Name: AI Creator Ops
Canonical domain: https://aicreatorops.com
Tagline: Build the creator business, not just the character.
Tone: sharp, practical, authoritative, operator-first, mildly opinionated.

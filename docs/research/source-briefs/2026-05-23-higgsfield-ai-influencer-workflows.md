## High-confidence primary-source findings

### 1. Higgsfield officially targets AI influencer and virtual character workflows

- Primary source: <https://higgsfield.ai/ai-influencer>
- The official Higgsfield AI Influencer Generator page says users can create a consistent digital influencer and generate social-ready videos for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts without appearing on camera.
- The page describes a three-step workflow: create the star, make it move, then generate/download ready-to-post video.
- It explicitly discusses consistent identity, advanced image generation, character style/personality, scenario control, makeup/imperfections/outfits, and social growth.
- Operator implication: public AI Creator Ops pages can cite Higgsfield directly for the fact that it positions itself as an AI influencer / virtual character tool, rather than only inferring the use case from YouTube tutorials.

### 2. Motion transfer and brand-collab language are official, but rights/disclosure still need review

- Primary source: <https://higgsfield.ai/ai-influencer>
- Higgsfield's AI influencer page says users can upload a video reference so a character performs the same moves, and it positions the feature around trending dances and viral challenges.
- The same page describes inserting brand products or props into a character's hands with realistic lighting and making posts shoppable.
- Operator implication: this is relevant to AI influencer and synthetic UGC workflows, but public guidance should pair it with rights review for reference videos, brand assets, likenesses, and platform disclosure requirements.

### 3. Pricing is subscription/credit-based and should be treated as plan- and promotion-sensitive

- Primary source: <https://higgsfield.ai/pricing>
- The official pricing page describes plans with monthly credits, concurrent video/image jobs, selected/all-model access, and plan-gated Supercomputer-related features.
- The page also describes unlimited-generation mechanics and promotional offers that vary by model, plan, and timing.
- Operator implication: store `pricing_model: subscription_and_usage_based`, but avoid quoting exact plan prices in durable public copy unless the page is freshly checked.

### 4. Higgsfield terms support commercial-use language but also give Higgsfield broad service and API discretion

- Primary source: <https://higgsfield.ai/terms-of-use-agreement>
- The extracted terms summary says the service includes the website, subdomains, resources, APIs made available by Higgsfield, and long-form video creation service.
- The terms state Higgsfield does not claim ownership of user inputs or outputs and does not restrict commercial use of outputs, while also stating inputs and outputs may be used to train, develop, enhance, evolve, and improve Higgsfield AI models and services.
- The terms also say Higgsfield may limit or modify API usage at its discretion, including network calls, file sizes, and other API characteristics.
- Operator implication: tool pages can mention commercial-use permissiveness only with caveats around terms, rights, model/service changes, and data/privacy preferences.

### 5. Trust, privacy, and Earn terms create material operator caveats

- Primary sources: <https://higgsfield.ai/trust>, <https://higgsfield.ai/privacy-policy>, <https://creators.higgsfield.ai/terms>
- The Trust page says content moderation is applied at model level, publicly shared Community content is screened, users and the public can report concerns, and users hold necessary rights for uploaded content and likenesses.
- The Privacy Policy says users should not provide confidential, sensitive, unlicensed proprietary, or biometric information through the service; it also discusses user-shared media, prompt/query data, public visibility of some shared content, and training/improvement uses.
- Higgsfield Earn terms describe regional eligibility, account/platform verification, automated submission review, campaign requirement checks, fraud/engagement manipulation restrictions, appeals, discretionary payments, and no guarantees around campaign availability or payout amounts.
- Operator implication: AI Creator Ops should not present Higgsfield Earn or AI influencer monetization as guaranteed income. It is a campaign/submission program with verification and discretionary review.

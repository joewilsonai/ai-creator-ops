# OnlyFans AI creator platform notes

Date checked: 2026-05-18
Platform record: `onlyfans`
Primary source IDs: `onlyfans-terms`, `onlyfans-help-ai-content`, `onlyfans-acceptable-use-policy`, `onlyfans-creator-center`

## Operator answer

OnlyFans is a major fan monetization platform, but it should be treated as high-risk for pure synthetic AI model or AI girlfriend businesses unless the operation is built around a verified creator, transparent AI disclosure, and documented rights to all likeness/media used.

## Source-backed points

- OnlyFans Terms of Service include an AI Generated Content clause requiring AI-generated content to comply with the Terms and be clearly and conspicuously captioned as AI Generated Content with a signifier such as `#ai` or `#AIGenerated`.
- OnlyFans Help search result for “Can I post AI content on OnlyFans?” states AI-generated, altered, or enhanced content may be shared only when it clearly features the verified Creator and users can tell the content was generated, altered, or enhanced by AI.
- OnlyFans Acceptable Use Policy remains the core content-risk review surface for adult content, consent, age/likeness issues, and account enforcement.
- OnlyFans Terms/search result material and official creator resources support the standard 20% platform-fee / 80% creator-share framing, but payout conditions and account terms still need ongoing review.

## Editorial implications

- Do not position OnlyFans as broadly AI-native in the same way Fanvue is currently documented.
- Use careful language: “high-risk unless verified creator + transparent AI labeling + rights documentation are in place.”
- Avoid unsupported income claims.

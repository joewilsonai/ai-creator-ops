## High-confidence primary-source findings

### Fanvue: AI creator labels are fan-visible and tied to refund expectations

Source: [Fanvue Help Centre — How do I know if a creator is AI?](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/14290142-how-do-i-know-if-a-creator-is-ai)

Findings:

- Fanvue defines an AI creator as a creator account where photos, videos, chat messages, or sometimes voice are generated by artificial intelligence.
- Fanvue says AI creators are a “fully recognised and verified category” and that every AI creator account goes through the same verification process as human creators.
- Fanvue says every AI creator has an AI tag displayed in the profile bio.
- Fanvue says AI-generated content is permitted under its Terms & Conditions Section 10.3, and that standard non-refundable terms apply when the AI label was visible before subscription.
- Fanvue asks users to report creators who appear AI-generated but have no label.

Operator implication:

- Fanvue is not merely tolerating AI creators; it is productizing them as a labeled account category. AI Creator Ops pages should distinguish this from platforms that only allow AI-assisted content featuring a verified human creator.
- Fanvue operators should treat visible profile labeling as part of the conversion funnel and compliance UX, not just a legal footnote.

### Fanvue creator Help Centre exposes AI creator operations as a first-class documentation area

Source: [Fanvue Help Centre — For Creators](https://help.fanvue.com/en/collections/4361688-for-creators)

Findings:

- Fanvue’s creator Help Centre includes a “Become an AI Creator on Fanvue” section.
- Listed articles include AI-content rules, AI-generated model definition, KYC/multiple accounts as an AI creator, AI Generated Content, AI Creator Coach, AI Analytics, and AI Messaging.
- The same creator help hub also includes payouts, settings, automated messages, lists, posts, scheduling, uploading content, promotions, and profile-link sharing.

Operator implication:

- The Fanvue page should link AI creator compliance, monetization, messaging, analytics, and social-funnel setup together. This is not a generic “fan platform” record; it is a platform record with unusually explicit AI creator operations docs.

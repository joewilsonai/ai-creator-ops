## Executive summary

Fanvue is the clearest primary-source match found this run for the current AI influencer / AI model / AI girlfriend monetization funnel. Unlike many market-signal pages that imply platform fit, Fanvue's own Help Centre explicitly says it welcomes AI creators and allows fully AI-generated content, but with meaningful constraints: clear AI disclosure, no deceptive/harmful AI media, age-appearance restrictions, copyright compliance, moderation review, and verification requirements when a real model/body is involved.

The operator takeaway is not “Fanvue is risk-free for AI creators.” It is: Fanvue is unusually explicit about allowing AI-generated creator media, while still treating disclosure, provenance, age appearance, co-creator verification, chargebacks, and payout review as operating controls.

YouTube and X signals continue to show a heavy 2026 tutorial market around creating AI influencers or AI models, promoting them on Instagram/TikTok-style surfaces, and funneling monetization to Fanvue. Those signals are useful for keyword and page-priority decisions, but earnings claims and “easy money” playbooks should remain excluded from public hard claims unless backed by platform/audited evidence.

## High-confidence primary-source findings

### 1. Fanvue explicitly welcomes AI creators and allows fully AI-generated content

Primary source: Fanvue Help Centre, “Is AI content allowed on Fanvue?”
URL: https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue

High-confidence evidence:

- Fanvue says it is “pleased to welcome AI creators” to its community.
- Fanvue says it allows fully AI-generated content on the platform.
- Fanvue also says it allows deepfaked/face-swapped content, but the real model behind the AI must be verified.
- Fanvue says content taken from other websites or creators without explicit authorization is not allowed.

AI Creator Ops implication:

- Fanvue should be treated as a high-priority fan-platform page and comparison entity for AI influencers, AI models, virtual influencers, and AI girlfriend brands. Its AI-friendliness can be described with primary-source support, but the page needs compliance caveats rather than hype.

### 2. Fanvue requires clear AI disclosure for AI-generated media

Primary source: Fanvue Help Centre, “Is AI content allowed on Fanvue?”
URL: https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue

High-confidence evidence:

- Fanvue says AI-generated media uploaded, posted, or shared on Fanvue must disclose that it is not real and was generated using AI.
- Fanvue says the disclosure must be clear and prominent.
- Fanvue gives examples of where disclosure may appear: profile bio, content caption, or watermark.
- Fanvue says failure to properly disclose may result in content removal and/or account suspension.

AI Creator Ops implication:

- Any Fanvue launch checklist should include a disclosure gate for profile bio, captions, and/or watermark strategy. Public guidance should avoid implying that AI characters can be passed off as real people.

### 3. Fanvue's AI rules add moderation, age-appearance, copyright, and non-deception constraints

Primary sources:

- Fanvue Help Centre, “Is AI content allowed on Fanvue?” — https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue
- Fanvue Help Centre, “AI Generated Content” — https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/8442803-ai-generated-content

High-confidence evidence:

- Fanvue says AI-generated media must not be used to create harmful or misleading content, and content intended to deceive or harm others is prohibited.
- Fanvue says AI content must pass a “Reasonable Person's Test” conducted by three or more members of its moderation team.
- Fanvue says a failed test results in content removal and multiple failures may result in permanent suspension.
- Fanvue says AI-generated media must not be of a real person other than the account owner.
- Fanvue says AI-generated media must not resemble or intend to resemble, visually or contextually, an individual under the age of 18.
- Fanvue says prompt text cannot be used to “prove” the age of AI-generated content.
- Fanvue says AI-generated media must not infringe copyright or ownership rights.
- Fanvue lists consequences that may include warning, removal, earnings deduction, suspension, or permanent ban.

AI Creator Ops implication:

- Fanvue’s risk model for AI creators should include “moderation/provenance risk,” not just “allowed vs banned.” Identity consistency workflows also need negative controls: no underage-coded visuals, no real-person impersonation, and no scraped or unauthorized likeness inputs.

### 4. Fanvue distinguishes AI-generated models from face-swaps/deepfakes involving real bodies

Primary source: Fanvue Help Centre, “What is an AI-Generated model?”
URL: https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538752-what-is-an-ai-generated-model

High-confidence evidence:

- Fanvue defines AI-generated models as virtual characters or avatars created using artificial intelligence.
- Fanvue says AI-generated models can look and behave like real people or entirely fictional beings.
- Fanvue says AI-generated models do not include deepfakes/face-swaps.
- Fanvue says if a model’s body is of a real person, even if the face is AI, that model is classed as a real model and must be verified through KYC.
- Fanvue says creators must disclose that an AI model is AI in their bio and cannot tell fans they are a real person in posts and chats.

AI Creator Ops implication:

- The site should distinguish between from-scratch synthetic characters, AI-enhanced real creators, and face-swap/deepfake workflows. Fanvue is more permissive than many platforms, but the verification boundary matters.

### 5. Fanvue’s standard creator earning rate is 80%, with 20% retained as platform fees

Primary source: Fanvue Policies, “Creator Earnings & Payouts”
URL: https://legal.fanvue.com/creator-earnings-payouts

High-confidence evidence:

- Fanvue says the standard Creator Earning Rate is 80% of gross revenue from paid-for services unless otherwise agreed in writing.
- Fanvue says the remaining 20% is retained as Platform Fees.
- Fanvue lists qualifying paid-for services including subscriptions, tips, pay-per-view messages, bundles, and custom content.
- Fanvue may offer temporary promotional earning rates, with eligibility/rate/duration published on-platform.
- Creator earnings may be adjusted for refunds, chargebacks, and fraud-related deductions.
- The standard pending period is seven days, but Fanvue says it may be extended up to 28 days depending on factors such as account verification and user activity.
- Fanvue aims to initiate valid payout requests within 10 business days, but policy notes delays may occur due to fraud/content investigations, banking delays, payment details, and related issues.

AI Creator Ops implication:

- Fanvue’s page can now cite an 80/20 standard split, but should note that promotional rates, holds, chargebacks, fraud reviews, and account status can change realized payout timing.

### 6. Fanvue payout help adds practical first-withdrawal requirements

Primary source: Fanvue Help Centre, “How to receive a payout on Fanvue”
URL: https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9515801-how-to-receive-a-payout-on-fanvue

High-confidence evidence:

- Fanvue says creators need a minimum available balance for withdrawal; the article references $20 but notes regional variation and says thresholds may range from $20 to $50.
- Fanvue says creators must have uploaded a minimum of five pieces of content.
- Fanvue says creators must verify any co-creators featured in content.
- Fanvue says first-time withdrawals are subject to a routine fraud check.
- Fanvue says requests are sent to the chosen payout method within 3–7 business days depending on method in the help article, while the legal earnings policy says Fanvue aims to initiate valid payout requests within 10 business days.

AI Creator Ops implication:

- Public copy should avoid oversimplified “instant payout” phrasing. Use a payout-readiness checklist: balance threshold, content minimum, co-creator verification, fraud review, payout method, and region.

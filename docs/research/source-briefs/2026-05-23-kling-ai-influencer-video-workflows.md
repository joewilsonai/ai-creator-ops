## Executive summary

Kling remains a credible tool-page and comparison-page priority for AI Creator Ops because the strongest evidence connects directly to a core operator problem: keeping a synthetic creator visually consistent once still images need to become short-form video. Primary sources support cautious claims that Kling/Kuaishou offers AI video/image generation, paid membership and credit-style services, commercial-use rights for paid members subject to restrictions, AI-generated-content labeling/watermark expectations, and a multi-image reference feature explicitly positioned around consistency.

The YouTube/X market signal is stronger than the official docs for the specific `AI influencer` use case: current creator tutorials repeatedly show a pipeline of reference images or Pinterest-style inspiration, ChatGPT/prompting, Kling image-to-video or Higgsfield/Kling workflows, then distribution to Instagram, TikTok, and Shorts. Treat those tutorial claims as workflow context, not authority for pricing, rights, disclosure, or API availability.

Recommended bounded update: improve the future `/tools/kling` page with a sourced section around "identity-to-video workflow" and a risk note that reference-image use requires rights/consent review. Do not make hard public claims about Kling 3.0 pricing, API endpoints, or exact plan-gated features until the official pricing/API pages can be reliably source-reviewed beyond JS-rendered pages.

## High-confidence primary-source findings

### 1. Kling/Kuaishou positions multi-image reference as a consistency feature

- Primary source: Kuaishou Technology investor-relations release, `Kuaishou Kling AI Launches “Multi-Image Reference” for More Consistent AI Video Generation`.
- The release says users can upload one or more reference images and combine them with text prompts to generate videos that preserve subject, style, and scene consistency.
- The release examples include person/object/scene references, scene/outfit control, and interactions between multiple subjects.
- Operator implication: this is directly relevant to AI influencer and virtual influencer continuity, where the character, wardrobe, setting, and props need to remain recognizable across clips.
- Caveat: the cited release is from 2025 and specifically mentions Kling AI 1.6. Use it as durable evidence that Kling has official consistency-oriented reference workflows, not as proof of every current 3.0/Omni capability.

### 2. Kling user terms emphasize lawful inputs/outputs, rights, and AI-generated-content labeling

- Primary source: Kling AI user service agreement page (`https://klingai.com/docs/user-policy`).
- The fetched agreement summary states the service is built on AI models for text, image, audio, and video generation.
- It forbids using Kling AI technology to infringe others' rights, including privacy, intellectual property, portrait/likeness, and other legal rights.
- It states the platform may add explicit and implicit labels to AI-generated/synthetic content according to legal requirements.
- It also warns non-members or users without written permission not to remove or alter Kling-brand watermarks and says generated output should be prominently identified as generated by the Kling AI model in relevant usage contexts.
- Operator implication: AI creator workflows using reference images must treat likeness/source-image rights as a production constraint, not just a prompting step.

### 3. Kling paid-service terms support cautious commercial-use language for members

- Primary source: Kling AI paid-service agreement (`https://klingai.com/docs/payment-policy`).
- The fetched agreement summary describes paid services including memberships, Inspiration Credits, standalone paid features, and automatic-renewal service rules.
- It lists member benefits including 1080p generation, watermark removal, priority/new-feature access, and a statement that commercial use of output content is not restricted for members, subject to legal use and a restriction against using outputs to develop or provide competing products/services against Kling AI.
- It states paid virtual-consumption goods are not refundable once opened and says Inspiration Credits are not currency, tokens, tickets, or prepaid certificates.
- Operator implication: public copy can say Kling has paid-service/member terms that address commercial output use, but should not simplify this into "all Kling outputs are commercially safe" without plan/status and rights review.

### 4. Official pages are JS-heavy; pricing/API details need cautious handling

- Source discovery found official developer/pricing/update pages, including `https://klingai.com/global/dev/pricing`, `https://kling.ai/dev/pricing`, and `https://app.klingai.com/global/dev/document-api/apiReference/updateNotice`.
- Direct fetches returned JS-rendered pages rather than stable extracted pricing/API tables.
- Operator implication: keep `api_available` for Kling under review unless a stable official API doc/page can be parsed and cited. Avoid exact public pricing/API claims from third-party summaries.

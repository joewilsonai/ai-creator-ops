## Operator takeaway

OnlyFans is a major fan monetization platform, but it should be treated as a high-control option for AI creator, AI model, and AI girlfriend business workflows. The official Terms now provide a clear AI-generated-content disclosure requirement, but the site still needs conservative handling around identity verification, collaborator verification, acceptable-use rules, and non-deceptive synthetic media presentation.

## Source-backed notes added

- OnlyFans Terms of Service state that AI-generated content must comply with the Terms and be clearly and conspicuously captioned as AI Generated Content with a signifier such as `#ai` or `#AIGenerated`.
- OnlyFans Creator Center says creators own 100% of their content and keep 80% of earnings.
- OnlyFans is adult-only, and creator onboarding includes identity/creator verification.
- Collaborations and content involving other people require collaborator disclosure/verification before posting.

## Editorial treatment

- Do not present OnlyFans as broadly “AI-friendly” without qualification.
- Compare it against Fanvue as a stricter, larger, more policy-sensitive fan-platform option.
- For AI companion or AI girlfriend operators, emphasize disclosure, verified account ownership, consent/provenance, and acceptable-use review before launch.
